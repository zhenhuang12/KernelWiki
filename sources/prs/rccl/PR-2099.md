## Summary

Adds an experimental GPU-Direct-Async (GDA) AllToAll path in RCCL implemented on top of rocSHMEM, gated by `RCCL_ROCSHMEM_ENABLE=1`. Targets MI300 (CDNA 3) hosts paired with Thor2 NICs.

## Why It Matters

Reports significant small-message AllToAll latency improvements over the default ring/Direct paths, which is the regime that MoE expert-parallel dispatch/combine cares about. Establishes the integration pattern for plugging rocSHMEM transport into an RCCL collective entry point.
