## Summary

Two changes on the gfx942 (CDNA 3) MLA ASM path: (1) rebuilds the BF16 `mla_qh8` kernel with the correct `group_segment_fixed_size=65536` LDS allocation, and (2) adds a new FP8 E4M3FNUZ `mla_qh8` kernel using `v_mfma_f32_16x16x32_fp8_fp8` (K=32, 18 K-iterations versus 36 for the BF16 K=16 path).

## Why It Matters

The BF16 fix unblocks correct execution of the QH=8 MLA decode kernel on MI300X; the FP8 variant halves the K-loop trip count by going from K=16 (BF16) to K=32 (FP8) MFMA. Together they make MLA decode at QH=8 viable for both BF16 and FP8 KV-cache configurations on CDNA 3.
