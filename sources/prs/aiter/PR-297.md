## Summary

Refines the fused-MoE block_n heuristic so it dispatches on the *effective* tokens-per-expert count rather than the raw length of `sorted_expert_ids`. For small M (decode-time) shapes the previous heuristic chose oversized block_n and wasted compute on padded expert rows; the new rule selects a smaller block_n.

## Why It Matters

Reports ~2x speedup (135us to 77us) on small-M MoE shapes typical of low-batch decoding. This is one of the canonical AITER examples of how MoE tile-scheduling decisions must account for expert load imbalance, not just total token count.
