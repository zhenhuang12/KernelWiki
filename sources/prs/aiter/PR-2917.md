## Summary

Adds a non-persistent MLA decode ASM kernel parameterized for sub_kv=64 and sub_qh=8 — the shape produced by DeepSeek-V3.2 / V4 with TP4 BF16 at seqlen=1. Targets both CDNA 3 (MI300X) and CDNA 4 (MI355X).

## Why It Matters

QH=8 is the worst case for MLA decode: it leaves a lot of MFMA throughput on the table because the query-head dimension is small. This non-persistent ASM variant becomes the reference for that regime; subsequent MLA tuning work (FP8 qh8, persistent variants) starts from this skeleton.
