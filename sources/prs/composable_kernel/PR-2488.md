## Summary

Adds the FP6 GEMM test parameterization for the MX (microscaling) GEMM family on CDNA 4. The test exercises `v_mfma_scale_f32_*_f8f6f4` with FP6 (E2M3 / E3M2) inputs and UE8M0 32-element block scales.

## Why It Matters

This is the test-side companion to the MX V3 pipeline FP6 enablement (PR #2481). Together they validate the CDNA 4 block-scaled MFMA path for FP6 weights, an intermediate point between FP8 and MXFP4 in the MX format family.
