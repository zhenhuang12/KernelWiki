## Summary

Wires the gfx950 (CDNA 4) matrix-core MFMA instruction set into the `01_fmha` forward kernel in ck_tile. The PR adds the new MFMA shapes available on CDNA 4 to the FMHA fwd policy and threads them through the pipeline.

## Why It Matters

This is the first CK-Tile FMHA forward variant to take advantage of CDNA 4 matrix cores, with reported 1.5x-1.7x performance uplift over the gfx942 MFMA shapes on the same kernel. Downstream FlashAttention-3-style pipelines (e.g. PR #2731) reuse the MFMA shape selection logic introduced here.
