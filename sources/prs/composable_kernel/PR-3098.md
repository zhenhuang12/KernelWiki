## Summary

Extends the CK MoE MXFP4 path on gfx950 with a `block_m=32` tile shape (matching the DeepSeek-style TP8 expert layout) and adds a new `moe_gemm2` v1 pipeline. Uses `v_mfma_scale_f32_*_f8f6f4` with UE8M0 block scales for MXFP4 expert weights.

## Why It Matters

DeepSeek-V3 / R1 style MoE deployments with TP8 expert sharding produce small per-expert M dimensions; the block_m=32 tile makes the CK MXFP4 grouped GEMM viable at that shape. The new `moe_gemm2` pipeline becomes the reference for the second (down) projection in the MoE fused stack on CDNA 4.
